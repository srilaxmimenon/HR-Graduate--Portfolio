How I work

Learning and development internship · 10+ programmes · 200+ participants

Situation

Multiple learning programmes involved learners, facilitators, HR stakeholders, venues, virtual arrangements and administrative requirements.

Challenge

Effective delivery required coordination across different stages of the learner journey while maintaining accurate programme information.

My role

I supported the operational coordination of learning programmes and acted as a point of contact for learners, facilitators and HR stakeholders.

Approach

  • Tracked programme and learner information.
  • Coordinated communications and reminders.
  • Supported attendance and nominee updates.
  • Coordinated practical delivery requirements.
  • Maintained programme and budget information.

Implementation

I supported Learning Management System administration, learner communications, attendance tracking, feedback collection, reporting and coordination with relevant stakeholders throughout programme delivery.

Outcome

I supported the delivery of 10+ corporate learning programmes reaching more than 200 participants, with reported on-time execution across the programme slate.

What I learned

Learning delivery is influenced by much more than the learning session itself. Clear communication, accurate information and well-organised administration can significantly shape the experience around learning.

Transferable capabilities

Challenge
